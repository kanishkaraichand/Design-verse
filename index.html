Welcome to the Verse

DesignVerse, organised by the Nirvana Design Society, brings together creativity, technology and curiosity under one roof. It is built for first-year students taking their first steps into the world of design.

Through competitions, hands-on workshops, an exhibition and talks from industry voices, you'll learn new skills, meet like-minded people and see how far an idea can go.

No experience needed, just curiosity.

What's Inside

Five unique dimensions crafted to jumpstart your creative journey.

Design Challenge

Get a theme, a time limit and a blank canvas. Create a poster, UI or concept and compete for the spotlight.

Innovation Sprint

Team up, brainstorm and pitch a creative solution to a real problem in just a few hours.

Workshops

Learn by doing in beginner-friendly sessions on UI/UX, Figma, and creative tools.

Design Exhibition

Walk through a gallery of student and society work and get inspired for your own.

Guest Talks

Hear from designers and creators about their journeys, and ask them anything. Speaker lineup to be announced.
